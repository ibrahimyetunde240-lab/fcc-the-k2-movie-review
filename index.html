Movie Review: The K2

The K2 is a South Korean action thriller drama that follows Kim Je-ha, a former soldier who becomes a bodyguard for Go An-na, the reclusive daughter of a powerful presidential candidate. As Je-ha becomes caught in a dangerous web of political ambition, secrets, betrayal, and revenge, he must protect An-na while confronting enemies from his own past.

Movie Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ (9.0/10)
